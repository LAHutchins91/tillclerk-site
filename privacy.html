Privacy Policy

Effective: September 14, 2026 · Contact: lawrence.a.hutchins@gmail.com

TillClerk is a conversational analytics app for small business owners. It is designed to be local-first: demo data, chats, business profile, boards, forms, and settings are stored on your device by default.

1. Who we are

TillClerk is developed by Lawrence Hutchins (“we,” “us”). This policy explains what information the iOS app may process and how it is used.

2. Information the app may process

  • Business profile (optional, user-entered): business name, DBA, type, owner name, email, phone, address, EIN, website, currency. Used only to personalize the app and autofill Forms on your device.
  • User content: chat questions and answers, KPI Board pins, Insights feedback, Documents, Schedules, Memories, Form fills, uploaded files (e.g. CSV/Excel/PDF/images) and connector demo sync datasets you choose to load.
  • Purchases: Apple In-App Purchase / subscription status for TillClerk Pro (processed by Apple; we receive entitlement signals via StoreKit, not your full payment card details).
  • Diagnostics (limited): standard OS crash / performance signals Apple may collect for App Store apps; TillClerk does not run third-party advertising SDKs or sell personal data.
  • Demo data: the Harbor Bean Co. sample dataset ships with the app for offline use and is not personal data about you.

3. How information is used

  • App Functionality — answer business questions, show verified metrics, pin KPIs, fill forms, manage schedules, and unlock Pro features.
  • Not used for tracking — TillClerk does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites for advertising.
  • No third-party advertising in the app.

4. Local storage vs optional network

By default, asks run with the on-device / offline engine against demo or locally loaded data. If you (or a future build you configure) enable an optional hosted ask endpoint, questions you submit to that endpoint may be transmitted to that service solely to return an answer. Connectors that require OAuth (POS, books, Drive, Slack, etc.) only sync when you connect them and credentials are configured; Free tier uses Harbor Bean demo data without live sync.

5. Photos & camera

If you grant Photo Library or Camera permission, TillClerk uses selected images for on-device analysis in chat (e.g. shelf/receipt/store photos). Images stay under your control on device unless you export or share them yourself.

6. Subscriptions

TillClerk Pro is an auto-renewable Apple subscription (com.lahutchins91.tillclerk.pro.monthly), typically with a 2-week free introductory offer then a monthly price shown in the App Store (e.g. $4.99/mo USD). Billing, cancellation, and refunds are handled by Apple. Manage or cancel in iOS Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions.

7. Data retention & deletion

Because most data is on-device, deleting the app removes local TillClerk data from that device (subject to any device backups you keep). In-app: More → Settings → Clear local data clears chats, insights, connectors, schedules, and related local state. Business profile can be reset to the Harbor Bean demo under Business profile. Apple retains subscription records per Apple’s policies.

8. Children’s privacy

TillClerk is intended for business owners and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes

We may update this policy as the product evolves. The effective date above will change when we do. Continued use after updates means you accept the revised policy.
