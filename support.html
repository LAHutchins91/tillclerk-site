Getting started (demo — no login)

TillClerk works offline with sample data for Harbor Bean Co. There is no login gate for App Review or first launch.

  • Open the app → complete onboarding (or tap Browse Discover first).
  • From Home / Discover, pick a starter or Space (Sales, Cash, Inventory, Customers, Ops).
  • Ask in Chat (e.g. “How were sales last week?”) and look for the Verified badge and “How TillClerk got this.”
  • Optional: pin answers to the Board, edit Business profile, try Forms, or open the Free vs Pro paywall.

TillClerk Pro subscription

  • Product ID: com.lahutchins91.tillclerk.pro.monthly
  • Typically 2 weeks free, then $4.99/month (or your local App Store price).
  • Auto-renewable unless canceled at least 24 hours before the current period ends.

Manage or cancel: iPhone Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions (or Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions). You can also open More → Settings → Manage Apple subscriptions in the app.

Restore purchases: More → Settings → Restore purchases (same Apple ID).

Account & data deletion

TillClerk is local-first and does not require a cloud account for demo use.

  • Clear local data: More → Settings → Clear local data (chats, insights, connectors, schedules, and related on-device state).
  • Reset business profile: Business profile → Reset to Harbor Bean demo.
  • Remove the app: deleting TillClerk from your device removes its local data (except anything kept in your own iCloud/device backups).
  • Canceling Pro does not erase local chats; clear local data or delete the app if you want data removed.
